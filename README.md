# The Norfolk Journal and Guide's crossword, 1925

The Norfolk Journal and Guide, the Black weekly of Norfolk, Virginia, was printing a titled
crossword on its society page by January 31, 1925. This is the Every Puzzle Project's record
of it: two sample pages so far. Part of the
[Every Puzzle Project](https://github.com/EveryPuzzleProject).

| Date | Puzzle |
|---|---|
| 1925-01-31 | "CROSS-WORD PUZZLE", "STEPPING STONES", "(Copyright, 1924.)" |
| 1925-02-07 | "CROSS-WORD PUZZLE", "THE FOUR ANGLES", "(Copyright, 1924.)", with the answer to last week's |

These were bought in. The titles are those of Nos. 1 and 3 of the numbered series that The
Hancock Democrat of Greenfield, Indiana, began on December 4, 1924
([hancockdemocrat](https://github.com/EveryPuzzleProject/hancockdemocrat)), printed here
about eight weeks later and without the numbers. The Pittsburgh Courier's puzzles in the
same type carry "(© by Western Newspaper Union.)"
([pittsburghcourier](https://github.com/EveryPuzzleProject/pittsburghcourier)), so the
series is very likely that syndicate's. The grids themselves haven't been compared.

## What isn't known

- **When the feature began and ended here.** January 31, 1925 is the earliest page saved,
  not a known start.
- **Whether the Guide skipped puzzles**: Nos. 1 and 3 on consecutive Saturdays suggests it
  did not print the series in order.

## Status (2026-11-19)

Two pages saved and indexed. Nothing has been read by the OCR pipeline. Clues and answers
are not typed in here.
